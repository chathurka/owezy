## Summary

Owners edit supported group fields (name) and archive groups. Archived groups leave active lists but remain readable for historical context. Soft deletes stay separate from archive.

## Functional Requirements

- Owner can PATCH group name.
- Owner can archive a group (`archived_at`).
- Archived groups excluded from `GET /v1/groups`.
- Members can still open overview of archived groups (read-only).
- Edits blocked when archived.
